💡 Expert Strategy Insights
  • Runway Length Correction is a guaranteed 10-mark numerical. Master the three-step sequence: Elevation → Temperature → Gradient (35% combined cap check).
  • Hauling capacity numerical appeared all 4 years. Know: F = μ × W_d; R_total = R₁ + Rg + Rc; set F = R to find train load or speed.
  • Superelevation formula: e = G·V²/(127·R). Practice 2° curve problems (R = 860 m) — they appear repeatedly.
  • Turnout sketches: practice drawing Right-Hand turnout labelling all 6 parts (Stock Rail, Tongue, Heel, Lead, Crossing/Frog, Check Rail).
  • Sleeper density MCQ appears in every Q1. Memorise: N = M + x; spacing S = L/N; ballast depth d = (S − w)/2.

📚 Module-by-Module Exam Strategy

MODULE 1 — Permanent Way & Geometric Design  HIGH  ~25 Marks

Heaviest module. Every part of the Q1 MCQ section draws from here.

🎯 MASTER (do not skip)

  • Superelevation formula e = G·V²/(127·R) + cant deficiency concept
  • Coning of wheels — behaviour on straight vs curved track with sketch
  • Sleeper density formula, ballast depth formula, spacing calculation
  • Creep — causes (wave action, percussion, temperature), effects, anchors
  • Gauges — BG/MG/NG dimensions + uni-gauge justification

📖 STUDY

  • Rail wear — classification (top/side/joint), allowable limits
  • Cross-section of permanent way sketch with all dimensions

❌ SKIP (never tested)

  • Manufacturing specs of rail sections, steel chemistry, heat treatment
Tip: The 2° BG curve superelevation problem (R = 860 m) repeated in 2023 and 2025. Memorise the answer pattern: e ≈ 100 mm at 80 km/h.
MODULE 2 — Track Construction, Points & Signals  MEDIUM  ~15 Marks

🎯 MASTER

  • Hauling capacity = μ × W_d; resistances: R₁, Rg, Rc formulas
  • Right-hand & Left-hand turnout labeled sketches (6 components)
  • Diamond crossing sketch

📖 STUDY

  • Signal hierarchy: Outer → Home → Starter → Advance Starter
  • Centralised Traffic Control (CTC) — working principle

❌ SKIP

  • Internal mechanics of interlocking frames; wood preservative chemistry
MODULE 3 — Airport Planning & Runway Design  HIGH  ~20 Marks

🎯 MASTER

  • 3-step runway correction: Elevation (+7% per 300 m) → Temperature (+1% per °C above standard) → Gradient (+20% per 1% effective gradient). 35% combined cap on elev+temp.
  • ART formula: ART = T_a + (T_m − T_a)/3
  • Standard temperature at elevation h: T_std = 15 − 0.0065h

📖 STUDY

  • Windrose Type I (direction + duration) vs Type II (direction + speed + duration)
  • Zoning laws and imaginary surfaces (conical, horizontal, transitional)
  • Pilot controls: Elevator (pitch), Rudder (yaw), Ailerons (roll)
Warning: Always apply corrections in the correct sequence and check the 35% cap. Many students apply the gradient correction to the wrong base length!
MODULE 4 — Taxiway Design & Pavement  MEDIUM  ~12 Marks

🎯 MASTER

  • Speed-based turning radius: R₁ = V²/(125·f)
  • Geometry-based radius (Horonjeff): R₂ = W_b² / [0.5(W_t − T) − S]
  • ICAO minimum = 120 m for subsonic jets; design R = max(R₁, R₂, 120)

📖 STUDY

  • Flexible vs Rigid pavement design principles for airports
  • Balanced field concept for jet aircraft engine failure
MODULE 5 — Tunnel Engineering  HIGH  ~18 Marks

🎯 MASTER

  • Soft ground methods: Forepoling, Needle Beam, Drift, Shield (TBM)
  • Ventilation: Natural vs Mechanical (Blow-in, Exhaust, Combined)
  • Advantages of tunnels over open cuts (6 points)

📖 STUDY

  • Geological investigations before tunnel construction
  • Hard rock methods: Heading & Bench, Full Face, Drift methods
  • Tunnel route selection criteria
