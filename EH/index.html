📚 Module-level Strategy Guide

Actionable guidelines to direct your revision time. Know what to Master, Skim, and Skip based on previous year frequency analysis.

Module 1: Introduction

Hydrologic cycle representation, System representation, Global Water Budget.
Weight: 5%

🎓 Master (Core Focus)

Basic schematic representation of the Hydrologic cycle (frequently asked in short notes).

📖 Skim (Secondary Focus)

Global water budget statistics, history and scope of hydrology.

⚠️ Skip (Never Asked)

Detailed non-examinable timeline details of historical systems.

Module 2: Weather & Precipitation

Humidity indices, formation mechanisms, network adequacy, average precipitation, FMC consistency.
Weight: 20%

🎓 Master (Core Focus)

Thiessen polygon, double mass curve records consistency testing, optimum rain gauge network density, dew point/humidity conversions.

📖 Skim (Secondary Focus)

Precipitation formation mechanisms (condensation, cooling, droplet growth), rain gauge site specifications.

⚠️ Skip (Never Asked)

AWS and radar measurement of rainfall detailed calculations.

Module 3: Infiltration

Infiltration capacity, Horton's infiltration curve, Infiltration Indices (phi/W).
Weight: 15%

🎓 Master (Core Focus)

Horton's infiltration decay parameter fitting and integration, $\Phi$-index calculations from storms with initial loss.

📖 Skim (Secondary Focus)

Double ring infiltrometer mechanism, factors affecting infiltration.

⚠️ Skip (Never Asked)

Flooding type infiltrometer mathematical calibration.

Module 4: Evapotranspiration

Meyer's equation, Water budget, Energy budget, Lysimeter.
Weight: 10%

🎓 Master (Core Focus)

Reservoir water budget weekly evaporation and seepage volume estimations.

📖 Skim (Secondary Focus)

Energy-budget method, Lysimeter operation, Cetyl alcohol evaporator inhibitor (MCQ favorite).

⚠️ Skip (Never Asked)

Thornthwaite and Blaney-Criddle complex empirical calculator curves.

Module 5: Runoff

Empirical formulae, Infiltration method, Hydrograph analysis, Rational method.
Weight: 10%

🎓 Master (Core Focus)

Surface runoff depth from mass curve of rainfall excess calculations, equivalent runoff coefficient.

📖 Skim (Secondary Focus)

Flow duration curve dependency representation, stream order and density parameters.

⚠️ Skip (Never Asked)

Hydrologic runoff empirical formulas for different soils.

Module 6: Hydrograph

Base flow separation, UH derivation/assumptions, S-curve, Synthetic UH, Instantaneous UH.
Weight: 20%

🎓 Master (Core Focus)

S-curve method unit hydrograph duration changes (4-hr to 2-hr or 1-hr), Snyder's synthetic unit hydrograph calculations (lag, peak, base width).

📖 Skim (Secondary Focus)

LTI assumptions of UH theory, base flow separation (straight line, 2-line, 3-curve) sketches, triangular UH catchment area calculations.

⚠️ Skip (Never Asked)

Advanced Instantaneous Unit Hydrograph (IUH) complex derivation equations.

Module 7: Estimation of Flood

Rational method, Flood frequency analysis (Gumbel\'s method, Log-Pearson III).
Weight: 10%

🎓 Master (Core Focus)

Gumbel's extreme value method predicting 100/200/500-year recurrence floods from partial parameters.

📖 Skim (Secondary Focus)

Rational method peak discharge for drainage conduits, storm duration matching concentration time ($t_c$).

⚠️ Skip (Never Asked)

Log-Pearson Type III complex tables computations.

Module 8: Flood Routing

Modified Pul\'s reservoir routing, Muskingum channel routing, St. Venant equations.
Weight: 10%

🎓 Master (Core Focus)

Muskingum routing table computations, coefficient derivations, wedge vs prism storage properties.

📖 Skim (Secondary Focus)

Modified Pul's reservoir routing steps, proof that peak outflow intersects inflow hydrograph.

⚠️ Skip (Never Asked)

Saint-Venant momentum equations complete derivations.
