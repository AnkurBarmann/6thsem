📚 Module-level Strategy Guide

Master / Skim / Skip per module.

Module 1: Introduction

Hydrologic cycle, Global Water Budget.
Weight: 5%

🎓 Master

Schematic representation of Hydrologic cycle.

📖 Skim

Global water budget statistics, history.

⚠️ Skip

Non-examinable timeline details.

Module 2: Weather & Precipitation

Humidity, rain gauges, network adequacy.
Weight: 20%

🎓 Master

Thiessen polygon, double mass curve, optimum gauge density, dew point.

📖 Skim

Precipitation mechanisms (cooling, condensation).

⚠️ Skip

AWS/radar detailed calculations.

Module 3: Infiltration

Horton's curve, infiltration indices.
Weight: 15%

🎓 Master

Horton's parameter fitting, Φ-index with initial loss.

📖 Skim

Double ring infiltrometer, factors affecting infiltration.

⚠️ Skip

Flooding-type infiltrometer calibration.

Module 4: Evapotranspiration

Water/Energy budget, Lysimeter.
Weight: 10%

🎓 Master

Reservoir water budget, seepage volume estimations.

📖 Skim

Energy budget, Lysimeter, Cetyl alcohol inhibitor.

⚠️ Skip

Thornthwaite, Blaney-Criddle complex calcs.

Module 5: Runoff

Empirical formulae, Rational method.
Weight: 10%

🎓 Master

Surface runoff from mass curve, equivalent runoff coefficient.

📖 Skim

Flow duration curve, stream order/density.

⚠️ Skip

Hydrologic empirical formulas for soils.

Module 6: Hydrograph

UH, S-curve, Synthetic UH.
Weight: 20%

🎓 Master

S-curve UH duration changes, Snyder's synthetic UH.

📖 Skim

LTI assumptions, base flow separation, triangular UH.

⚠️ Skip

Advanced IUH derivations.

Module 7: Estimation of Flood

Gumbel, Log-Pearson III.
Weight: 10%

🎓 Master

Gumbel EVD for 100/200/500-yr floods.

📖 Skim

Rational method, time of concentration.

⚠️ Skip

Log-Pearson Type III tables.

Module 8: Flood Routing

Muskingum, Pul's, St. Venant.
Weight: 10%

🎓 Master

Muskingum routing tables, wedge vs prism storage.

📖 Skim

Pul's reservoir routing, peak outflow proof.

⚠️ Skip

Saint-Venant derivations.
